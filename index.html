AML Transaction Monitoring and Case Investigation Agent

Sep 2026 – present. Built from scratch on the SAML-D dataset.

PyTorch GeometricXGBoostLangGraphQdrantRAGASFastAPIReact
View repository →

The problem

Bank investigators get a flood of transaction alerts and have to read each case by hand to decide what is real laundering.

How I am solving it

  • An account graph of 175,888 nodes and 1,869,565 edges, with a chronological 70/15/15 split and leakage-safe features such as 7-day and 30-day velocity.
  • A rule engine for structuring, velocity, amount anomaly and fan patterns. Structuring is strongest, at 10.1x lift on the test split.
  • An XGBoost baseline and a GraphSAGE edge classifier. The baseline scores near-perfect (ROC-AUC 0.996), which matches published SAML-D results, so I documented dataset separability as a limitation.
  • Next: the LangGraph investigation agent, retrieval over evidence, and the React case dashboard.

The problem

Financial PDFs hold tables and charts that OCR flattens or breaks, especially on blurry or rotated scans, and the answers built on top of it go wrong.

How I solved it

  • A visual RAG pipeline that skips OCR. ColPali patch embeddings of PDF pages go into Qdrant, searched with native multi-vector MaxSim.
  • A vision-language answering layer over the retrieved page images, with a reproducible Precision@3 harness that compares it to a text-extraction baseline on clean and degraded documents.
  • On a real annual report, visual RAG matched the text baseline under degraded conditions (88.2% Precision@3). Text extraction stayed stronger on clean, digital-native PDFs.

Applied ML | Sep 2026

Customer Churn Prediction

Churn model and retention strategy for a telecom dataset.

XGBoostSHAPScikit-learnPandas
View repository →

The problem

A telecom company loses revenue when customers leave, and needs to know who is likely to go and what to do about it.

How I solved it

  • An end-to-end pipeline on 7,043 customers. Tuned XGBoost reached 0.8386 ROC-AUC, with a 95% bootstrap interval of 0.816 to 0.861.
  • An F2-score decision threshold that lifted churner recall from 80.5% to 89.6%.
  • SHAP analysis of the main churn drivers and three risk tiers, which put $679,646 of annual revenue in scope and $49,971 in the protectable tier.

Principles

How I work

Habits that show up in every project above.

Compare against a baseline

A new retrieval method only counts if it beats a simple one on the same questions. VisionExtract tests against plain text extraction for that reason.

Test on bad inputs

Real documents are blurry, rotated and noisy. I score systems on degraded pages as well as clean ones.

Explain the model

For credit and churn work I use SHAP, so every score comes with the drivers behind it and a business reading of them.
